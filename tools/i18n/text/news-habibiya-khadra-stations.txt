en: Habibiya serves Shaab, Sadr 1, Sadr 2, Ghadeer and New Baghdad. Khadra, in Karkh, serves Ghazaliya, Amiriya and nearby areas. Both stations now end the discharge of sewage into nearby drainage channels.
