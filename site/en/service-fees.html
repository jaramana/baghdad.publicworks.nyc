Service · Fees and business

Municipal fees

The Mayoralty charges a trade fee on shops and businesses, an advertising fee on signs, and a cleaning fee. Look up what you owe with your account number, then pay at your district office.

  • Who pays

    Shops and businesses

    And owners of advertising signs

  • Where to pay

    District office

    No online payment yet

  • When

    Once a year

    Illustrative

The three kinds of fee come from the Mayoralty's current website. The account lookup, its result, the amounts and the dates are illustrative.

What do you owe?

Printed on your last receipt. Don't know it? Your district office can give it to you.

The fees due on your account appear here.

Kinds of fee

  • Trade fee

    On every shop, office or workshop inside the Mayoralty's boundary. It depends on the kind of business and where it is.

  • Advertising fee

    On every sign or advertisement that faces the street. Based on its size and position.

  • Cleaning fee

    For waste collection from shops and businesses.

Paying

  1. Find the amountWith your account number above.
  2. Pay District officeAt the collection section, in cash or by card where available.
  3. Keep the receiptIt has your account number for next year.

Why we don't publish lists of names

The Mayoralty's website used to publish lists of fee payers with their names and businesses, municipality by municipality. This site doesn't. You see what you owe with your own account number, and no one else can see it.

Pay at your district office

Sunday to Thursday, 8:30 am – 2:30 pm.
