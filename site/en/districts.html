Mayoralty of Baghdad

Districts

The Mayoralty serves the city through 14 municipalities, six on the Karkh side and eight on the Rusafa side, plus the Green Zone and Airport Road municipality. Your municipality is your nearest office, and it is where your reports go.

Outside the Mayoralty's boundary?

The districts around the city, such as Abu Ghraib, Tarmiyah, Mahmudiyah and Mada'in, come under Baghdad Governorate, not the Mayoralty. If your property or your problem is there, the Governorate or the district's own municipality is responsible.
