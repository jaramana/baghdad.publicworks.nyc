Every project here is real and sourced. "Status unverified" means the sources are old or disagree, so the status may have changed.

Streets, the river and old Baghdad

The riverfront and the historic centre on the Rusafa bank.

  • Abu Nuwas corniche

    Rebuilding the riverside corniche (over $6 million in 2023) and widening about 8 km of street from Karrada Martyrs Park to Eagles Square. A later report found parts of the street neglected again.

    Sources: Arab Urban Development Institute · IraqiNews · Shafaq News

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad ("Baghdad Ajmal"); the street package by the Ministry of Construction and Housing
    Status
    Phase 1 open October 2025; street package over 88% done
    Status verified

  • Rashid Street rehabilitation

    Restoring facades and infrastructure on Baghdad's oldest modern street. Phase 2 covered about 3.5 km and 183 buildings.

    Sources: Channel 8 · Iraq Business News

    Runs it
    Prime Minister's Office, Central Bank of Iraq and the Iraqi Private Banks League
    Status
    Phase 3 under way since 2025
    Status verified

  • Mutanabbi Street

    Stone paving, lighting and painted facades on the booksellers' street. Phase 1 of the "Old Baghdad" programme.

    Source: The National

    Runs it
    Central Bank of Iraq and the Iraqi Private Banks League
    Status
    Reopened late December 2021
    Status verified

  • Saray–Qishla Street

    Phase 2 of "Old Baghdad": facades and a pedestrian zone between the Saray and the Qishla.

    Source: IraqiNews

    Runs it
    The "Old Baghdad" programme
    Status
    Under way or complete
    Status unverified no clear date

  • Baghdad heritage centre

    A centre to protect and record the city's heritage buildings, ordered by the Prime Minister.

    Source: Shafaq News

    Runs it
    Prime Minister's Office
    Status
    Ordered
    Status verified

Parks and trees

Baghdad has lost much of its green space to building. These projects add it back.

  • Baghdad Plants

    A campaign to plant one million trees across the city.

    Source: The New Region

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad
    Status
    Under way since March 2025
    Status verified

  • Zawraa Park rehabilitation

    Rebuilding a 3 km² park on a former army camp in Karkh, in three phases costing about $60 million.

    Sources: Wikipedia · IraqiNews

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad
    Status
    Reopened reported November 2025
    Status unverified area verified

  • Green Belt, Mansour to Abu Ghraib

    A belt of trees on 940 donums (about 235 hectares), watered with treated wastewater.

    Source: IraqiNews

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad
    Status
    Reported over 95% complete
    Status unverified the report's dates conflict

  • Baghdad Sustainable Forests

    About a million trees in 28 forests on the former Rashid military camp, designed by Gensler.

    Sources: Iraq Business News · designboom

    Runs it
    National Investment Commission
    Status
    Launched December 2024
    Status verified

Getting around

Rail plans and the road programme. The overpasses are national projects, not the Mayoralty's.

  • Baghdad Metro

    Seven lines, about 148 km and 64 stations. A consortium including Systra, SNCF, Alstom, Talgo and SENER was chosen in July 2024. HSS Engineers manages the project under a $316 million contract.

    Sources: Railway Gazette · Iraq Business News · MEED

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad and the National Investment Commission
    Status
    Under review since July 2025; MEED reports a retender and stalled talks
    Status unverified history verified

  • Baghdad Elevated Train

    About 20 km of elevated railway with 14 stations. Alstom and Hyundai E&C signed a letter of intent in 2020.

    Source: Alstom

    Runs it
    Ministry of Transport
    Status
    No construction found
    Letter of intent verified

  • Traffic-relief package

    New overpasses and bridges across the city to ease congestion, including the Qadisiyah overpass and a second Jadriya bridge in Karrada.

    Sources: Iraq Business News, Oct 2025 · Iraq Business News, Mar 2023

    Runs it
    Ministry of Construction and Housing, with the Prime Minister's Office
    Status
    Several overpasses open October 2025; others under way
    Status verified

Planning and housing

The city's master plan and its largest new neighbourhood.

  • Baghdad Comprehensive Development Plan 2030

    The city's master plan for land use, transport, environment and river transport, prepared by Khatib & Alami with World Bank funding.

    Sources: Arab Urban Development Institute · JEASIQ paper

    Runs it
    Mayoralty of Baghdad
    Status
    Approved 2022 per one source; a paper says stage 4 is still under discussion
    Status unverified the plan's existence is verified

  • Bismayah New City

    100,000 homes on about 1,830 hectares, 10 km south-east of the city, planned for 600,000 people.

    Sources: Kunhwa Engineering · Iraq Business News

    Runs it
    National Investment Commission; built by Hanwha
    Status
    Resumed 2024, after a 16-month pause
    Status verified
