Mayoralty of Baghdad

About the Mayoralty and this site

What the Mayoralty does and doesn't do, who is responsible for what in Baghdad, how to reach us, and how this site works and what it keeps about you.

What the Mayoralty does

The Mayoralty of Baghdad (Amanat Baghdad) is the capital's municipal government. The Mayoralty of Baghdad Law, No. 16 of 1995, sets its duties inside the city's planned boundary:

  • water and sewerage
  • roads and bridges inside the city
  • street cleaning and waste collection
  • parks and tree planting
  • building permits and land use
  • municipal fees

It works through 15 municipalities close to where you live, and central departments that each handle one service. It also runs the Baghdadi Museum, the radio station Sawt Baghdad and a newspaper of the same name.

Who does what

Several bodies work in Baghdad, and it isn't always clear which one you need. This table sums it up.

How work is split between the Mayoralty and the Governorate in the surrounding districts has not been checked in detail. If you report a problem that isn't ours, we pass it to the body responsible and tell you.

Who leads it and how it is organised

The Mayor of Baghdad leads the Mayoralty. The Mayor is appointed by the federal government, not elected. The current Mayor is Eng. Ammar Moussa Kadhim, in office since 2022. Three deputy mayors cover municipal, administrative and technical affairs.

Its departments include:

  • Baghdad Water
  • Baghdad Sewerage
  • Solid Waste and Environment
  • Parks and Afforestation
  • Designs
  • Projects
  • Planning and Follow-up
  • Real Estate
  • Audit and Control
  • Legal
  • Administration and Finance
  • Relations and Media

Two state companies belong to it: the Water Projects Execution Company and the Ibn Rushd General Company.

The Mayor's name and tenure are from the Mayoralty's own news posts and Wikipedia. The department list is from Arabic Wikipedia and links on the Mayoralty's current website, and is not the official organisation chart.

Tenders and auctions

Tenders are contracts the Mayoralty puts out to bid, such as building works or supplies. Auctions are sales and leases of the Mayoralty's own property and assets. Both are published on the unified national tenders platform.

See tenders on itp.iq ↗

The Mayoralty's planning rules and the 2030 development plan are also published as documents for architects, developers and contractors.

How search works

Search understands your request in your own words, then points you to a page on this site or a form that is already filled in. It uses AI, within clear limits:

  • It answers only from the Mayoralty's pages, and names the page each answer comes from.
  • If no page matches your request, it says so instead of guessing.
  • It gives no legal opinion and makes no decision on your application.
  • It does not link what you type to your name or number.

If it gets an answer wrong, tell us. Staff write and review every page it points you to.

Accessibility

We want everyone in Baghdad to be able to use this site, including people who use a screen reader, enlarge the text or have an old phone.

  • We work to the WCAG 2.2 AA standard.
  • Pages read without JavaScript. Maps and search need it, and every map has a text or table alternative.
  • Arabic comes first and reads right to left. The English version is written with care, not machine-translated.
  • Every link says where it takes you. No "click here".

Found something in your way? Tell us and we reply within five working days.

Privacy

  • When you report a problem we keep its type, place, photo and description. Your phone number is optional, and we use it only to send updates.
  • Report photos are never published. The public map shows the kind of problem and its rough location.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers. Visit statistics stay off until you agree to them.
  • We don't publish the names or businesses of fee payers. Why
