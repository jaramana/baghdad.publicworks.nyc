Service · Building and property

Sewer connection

If you build a new home or shop, it has to be connected to the sewer network. You start on the Ur portal, then visit your district office to have your documents checked.

  • Where to apply

    Ur portal, then the office

    Needs checks and a visit

  • Time

    About two weeks

    After the inspection · illustrative

  • Who runs it

    Baghdad Sewerage Department

    A department of the Mayoralty

The service on the Ur portal is verified, and its page says it needs checks and an in-person visit. The documents, steps and time are illustrative.

Do you need a connection?

You need one for every new building not yet on the network. You also need one when a building's use changes so that it drains more, for example when a home becomes a restaurant.

Don't connect a building to the network yourself. An unpermitted connection makes the whole street overflow, and it is an offence.

What you need

  • Building permitOr proof that the building is permitted. About building permits
  • A recent property statementGet a property statement
  • Unified national ID cardThe owner's.
  • Internal drainage planShows where the pipes leave the building.

Steps

  1. Apply Ur portalChoose "Sewer pipe connection" and upload your documents. You get an application number.
  2. Visit your district office District officeBring the original documents and your application number so they can be checked.
  3. Site inspectionThe sewer team decides where to connect and how long the pipe will be.
  4. Pay the fees Ur portalBased on the length of the connection and the pipe size.
  5. ConnectionDone by the Sewerage Department, or by a licensed contractor it supervises.
