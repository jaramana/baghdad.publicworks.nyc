Service · Building and property

Property statement

A property statement is a Mayoralty document that sets out a property's position, size, use and any fees owed on it. You request it online on the Ur portal and receive it there.

  • Where to apply

    Ur portal

    You need an Ur account

  • Time

    About 3 working days

    Illustrative

  • Cost

    A fixed fee

    Shown before you pay · illustrative

Where to apply on the Ur portal is verified. What the statement contains, the documents, the time and the cost are illustrative until they are checked with the Real Estate Department.

When do you need one?

  • Before you sell or buy a property.
  • To divide an inheritance.
  • To apply for a building permit.
  • To open a water account or move one to your name.

A property statement is not a title deed. The deed comes from the Real Estate Registration Department of the Ministry of Justice. The statement comes from the Mayoralty.

What you need

  • Property and plot numbersBoth are written on the title deed.
  • A copy of the title deedClear, both sides.
  • Unified national ID cardThe owner's, or a power of attorney if someone else applies.

Steps

  1. Apply Ur portalChoose "Open a property statement" and enter the property and plot numbers.
  2. Pay the fee Ur portalThe amount is shown before you pay.
  3. Receive the statementIt arrives as a file on Ur. Print it or send it to whoever asked for it.

Personal statement

A personal statement lists the properties registered in your name and what is owed on them. You also request it on the Ur portal, with the service "Request a personal statement".

Start your application

Applying takes about 10 minutes if you have the deed in front of you.

Apply on the Ur portal ↗

No account? Create an Ur account

Prefer to go in person? Apply at your district office during working hours.
