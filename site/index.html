Amanat Baghdad — home page directions

Newest first. Same core content in each; tone and layout change. Private study, local only.

  1. Report a problem · flow

    Four short steps with a map pin, optional photo, review and a receipt with a case number. Search answers and home links pre-select the problem type.

  2. Track a report

    Case lookup without an account, and a timeline from received to closed.

  3. Service page · Building permit

    First service template, built on the chosen home direction. Facts strip, guide with checklist and numbered steps, sticky start card that hands off to Ur, related services and page feedback.

  4. I · Blend, revised (current)

    The B + C blend with green kept as primary but never filled: white sticky glass masthead between green lines, outline buttons, green rules. The Tigris band is 3:1 with the search card and Today ledger over its lower edge. Adds the WhatsApp report block and fixed answer slot. City photos are 3:2 and equal.

  5. H · Seal colors as lines

    White sticky glass masthead framed by an indigo line on top and a teal hairline below. The Tigris returns as its own 3:1 band, with nothing on top of it. Indigo is the ink and small buttons; teal is the accent line. The report block and footer are framed by lines instead of filled. All other photos are 3:2 and equal width.

  6. G · Seal palette (filled)

    Type-led hero with no photo. Colors from the Mayoralty seal, with a switch between an indigo primary and a teal primary. Green is dropped from the brand. Sticky glass masthead that carries the nav and the "official site" line; the separate banner is gone.

  7. F · Synthesis (Editorial + Blend)

    The Blend's green masthead, seal and Kufi headings. The Tigris photo sits clear, with one white panel straddling its lower edge that holds the Naskh question, search and typeset ledger. Below it are Editorial's ruled services list, green report block with WhatsApp and "منطقتي" picker, then the Blend's two-photo City section.

  8. D · Editorial

    Light paper, type-led, almost no boxes. Naskh display type and ink rules. Green only in the wordmark and the report block. One live count in a typeset ledger.

  9. E · Grounded (rejected)

    Brick-textured green masthead, Reem Kufi display type, a framed Tigris photo beside the search, and a Today strip. Soft cards, no gradient wash.

  10. Earlier pick · B + C blend

    B's green masthead, seal, Kufi headings and Today ledger, set over C's Tigris photo. The City section uses C's two photos on B's deep green band, with its headline in Naskh.

  11. A · The Counter (الشبّاك)

    Restrained, GOV.UK-like. White page, black type, green accent, IBM Plex Sans Arabic. No photo until the City section.

  12. B · The Seal (الختم)

    Authoritative. Deep green band with a larger seal and a sand accent. Kufi headings. The Today card sits beside the search as a ledger.

  13. C · The River (النهر)

    Warm and visionary. Full-bleed Tigris photo behind a white search card. Naskh headings, teal accent, a larger City story.
