Service · Building and property

Building permit

You need a building permit before you build, or add to a building, on property inside the Mayoralty's boundary. You apply online on the Ur portal and follow your application there.

  • Where to apply

    Ur portal

    You need an Ur account

  • Time

    About 30 days

    Once your documents are complete · illustrative

  • Cost

    Depends on area and use

    Worked out at review · illustrative

The documents, steps and times on this page are illustrative until they are checked with the department. Where to apply on the Ur portal is verified.

Do you need a permit?

You need one to:

  • build on empty land
  • add a floor, a room or an annex
  • change the front of a building that faces the street
  • change how a building is used, for example from home to shop

You don't need one for inside repairs that leave the structure and the front untouched.

The full rules are in the Building Permits Regulation, No. 2 of 2016.

If your property is outside the Mayoralty's boundary, in the surrounding districts, Baghdad Governorate may be responsible. Check who is responsible

What you need

Have these ready before you start. You upload clear photos of each on the Ur portal.

  • A recent property statementIssued on the Ur portal. Get a property statement
  • Architectural and structural drawingsCertified by a licensed engineer or engineering office.
  • Unified national ID cardThe owner's, or a power of attorney if someone else applies.
  • A photo of the siteTaken from the street, showing the buildings next to it.

Steps

  1. Get your documents readyThe list above.
  2. Apply Ur portalSign in, choose "Building permit (all types)" and upload your documents. You get an application number.
  3. Drawings reviewThe department reviews the drawings and may ask for changes through Ur.
  4. Site inspectionSomeone from your municipality visits the property. You are told the date.
  5. Pay the fees Ur portalThe fees appear in your application after review.
  6. Receive the permitIt is issued online. Post it on the site for as long as work goes on.

After you apply

Follow your application on the Ur portal with its number.

The Mayoralty may ask you to rate the service afterwards, including how easy it was to follow your application without visiting the office.

Start your application

Applying takes about 20 minutes if your documents are ready.

No account? Create an Ur account

Prefer to go in person? Apply at your district office during working hours.
