Municipal fees

The Mayoralty charges a trade fee on shops and businesses, an advertising fee on signs, and a cleaning fee. Look up what you owe with your account number, then pay at your district office.

  • Who pays

    Shops and businesses

    And owners of advertising signs

  • Where to pay

    District office

    Payment in person only

  • When

    Once a year

    Illustrative

The account lookup, the amounts and the dates on this page are illustrative.

Look up your fees

The account number is printed on your last receipt. Your district office can also provide it.

Kinds of fee

  • Trade fee

    Applies to every shop, office or workshop inside the Mayoralty's boundary. The amount depends on the business type and location.

  • Advertising fee

    Charged on every sign or advertisement facing the street, according to its size and position.

  • Cleaning fee

    For waste collection from shops and businesses.

The fees are set by the Municipal Revenues Law, No. 1 of 2023. Household waste collection is free of charge.

Paying

  1. Find the amountWith your account number above.
  2. Pay District officeAt the collection section during office hours, in cash or by card where available. Find your district office
  3. Keep the receiptIt shows your account number for future payments.
