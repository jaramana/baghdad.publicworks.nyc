Using this website

What this website keeps about you, how we make it work for everyone, and how search finds its answers.

Privacy

  • When you report a problem we keep its type, place, photo and description. Your phone number is optional, and we use it only to send updates.
  • Report photos are never published. The public map shows the kind of problem and its rough location.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers. Visit statistics stay off until you agree to them.
  • We don't publish the names or businesses of fee payers. Why

Accessibility

We want everyone in Baghdad to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, enlarge the text or have an old phone.

  • We work to the WCAG 2.2 AA standard.
  • Pages read without JavaScript. Maps and search need it, and every map has a text or table alternative.
  • Arabic comes first and reads right to left. The English version is written with care, not machine-translated.
  • Every link says where it takes you. No "click here".

Found something in your way? Write to info@amanatbaghdad.gov.iq and we reply within five working days.

How search works

Search understands your request in your own words, then points you to a page on this website or a form that is already filled in. It uses AI, within clear limits:

  • It answers only from the Mayoralty's pages, and names the page each answer comes from.
  • If no page matches your request, it says so instead of guessing.
  • It gives no legal opinion and makes no decision on your application.
  • It does not link what you type to your name or number.

Staff write and review every page it points you to. If it gets an answer wrong, write to info@amanatbaghdad.gov.iq.
