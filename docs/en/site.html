Using this website

Read about the information this website collects, its accessibility features and how its search works.

Privacy

  • When you report a problem we keep its type, place, photo and description. Your phone number is optional, and we use it only to send updates.
  • Report photos are never published.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers. Visit statistics stay off until you agree to them.

Accessibility

We want everyone in Baghdad to be able to use this website, including people who use a screen reader, enlarge the text or have an old phone.

  • We aim to meet the WCAG 2.2 AA standard. The site has not yet been tested against it.
  • Pages read without JavaScript. Search, the report form, report tracking, the fee lookup and maps need it.
  • You can report a problem by typing the address instead of using the map. District maps repeat their alerts and works as text.
  • Arabic comes first and reads right to left.

Found something in your way? Write to info@amanatbaghdad.gov.iq with the page and the problem. You can also call the hotline, 5628, 8:30 am – 2 pm.

How search works

Search looks through the titles, summaries and key words of the pages on this website, and lists the pages that match, best match first.

  • It searches this website only.
  • It does not link what you type to your name or number.

Can't find what you need? Browse all services, or call the hotline, 5628, 8:30 am – 2 pm.
