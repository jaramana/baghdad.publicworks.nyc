About the Mayoralty

Leadership and structure

Who leads the Mayoralty, which office answers to whom, and the email for each one.

The Mayor

Eng. Ammar Moussa Kadhim has been Mayor of Baghdad since 2022. The Mayor is appointed by the federal government, not elected.

Three deputy mayors share the work below the Mayor: municipal affairs, technical affairs and administrative affairs. A few offices answer to the Mayor directly.
