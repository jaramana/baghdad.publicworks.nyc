Leadership and structure

Who leads the Mayoralty, which office reports to whom, and the email for each one.

Mayor of Baghdad

Eng. Ammar Moussa Kadhim

In office since 2022. The Mayor is appointed by the federal government, not elected.

Three deputy mayors oversee municipal, technical and administrative affairs. Some offices report directly to the Mayor.
