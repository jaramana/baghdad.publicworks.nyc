Water, sewage, waste, street lights, potholes

On the website

Add a photo, pin the place on the map and choose the problem. It takes two minutes.

Start a report

On WhatsApp

Send a photo and your location to the Mayoralty's number. You get a report number and every update in the same chat.

Open the chat

Verified number in the name “Mayoralty of Baghdad”. We never ask for payment on WhatsApp.
