Tenders and auctions

Tenders invite bids for Mayoralty contracts, such as building works and supplies. Auctions offer Mayoralty property for sale or lease. Each notice lists its closing date and links to the full terms.

Bidding

Enquiries about a notice should be sent to the Public Contracts Department, quoting the notice number: alukud@amanatbaghdad.gov.iq

The national tenders platform, itp.iq ↗, lists notices from all ministries and provinces, including the Mayoralty.

Sales and leases are governed by the State Property Sale and Lease Law, No. 21 of 2013.
