Property statement

A property statement is a Mayoralty document that sets out a property's location, size, use and any fees owed on it. You request it online on the Ur portal and receive it there.

  • Where to apply

    Ur portal

    You need an Ur account

  • Time

    About 3 working days

    Illustrative

  • Cost

    A fixed fee

    Shown before you pay · illustrative

Start your application

The application takes about 10 minutes. Have your title deed ready.

Apply on the Ur portal ↗

An Ur account is required. Create an Ur account

You can also apply in person at your district office during working hours.

What the statement contains, the documents, the time and the cost in this guide are illustrative.

When a statement is required

  • Before you sell or buy a property.
  • To divide an inheritance.
  • To apply for a building permit.
  • To open a water account or transfer one to your name.

A property statement is different from a title deed. Title deeds are issued by the Real Estate Registration Department of the Ministry of Justice.

What you need

  • Property and plot numbersBoth are written on the title deed.
  • A copy of the title deedA clear copy of both sides.
  • Unified national ID cardThe owner's, or a power of attorney if someone else applies.

Steps

  1. Apply Ur portalChoose "Open a property statement" and enter the property and plot numbers.
  2. Pay the fee Ur portalThe amount is shown before you pay.
  3. Receive the statementThe statement is issued as a file on Ur. Print it or send it to the office that requested it.

Personal statement

A personal statement lists the properties registered in your name and what is owed on them. You also request it on the Ur portal, with the service "Request a personal statement".
