Population by subdistrict

Results of the general population census of 25 November 2024, with 2009 estimates for comparison. The figures cover all of Baghdad Governorate, including districts outside the Mayoralty's boundary.

Subdistricts are statistical units. Their boundaries do not match those of the 15 municipalities, so subdistrict figures cannot be added together to give a municipality's population.
