    Mayoralty and the Federation of Arab Engineers agree to work together on urban development

    A memorandum of understanding covering sustainable development and city planning.

  • 19 Oct 2026 · Parks

    New 32,000 m² park opens in Kadhimiya

    Imamain al-Jawadain Park opened on 17 October.

  • 6 Oct 2026 · Parks

    Campaign to plant 190,000 trees starts in every municipality

    Trees will be planted along streets, in squares and at other public sites across the city.

  • 6 Oct 2026 · Parks

    Saqlawiya Garden opens in Ghazaliya

    One of several public gardens opened during the planting campaign, in Mansour municipality.

    Fountain opens on the Tigris at the Utaifiya corniche

    Opened on 1 October for National Sovereignty Day.

    Household waste collection is free of charge

    Requests for payment should be reported to 5628 or through the Citizen Voice app, with the truck's number.

  • 29 Sep 2026 · Works

    Drains and pumping stations to be ready before the rains

    The Mayor ordered every department to finish its rain-season preparations.

  • 29 Sep 2026 · Works

    Mansour municipality moves into a new building

    The first of the Mayoralty's model municipality offices.

  • 14 Sep 2026 · Services

    Work begins on one electronic system for building permits

    The system will link the Mayoralty's records and reduce the steps in the permit process on Ur.

  • 6 Sep 2026 · Services

    Citizen Voice complaints app upgraded

    The app now sorts complaints, sends each to the responsible office and follows it until it is closed. It will also be available inside the SuperQi app.
