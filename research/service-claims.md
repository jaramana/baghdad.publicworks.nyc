# Service claims register

Every factual claim on the English service and reporting pages, with its status. Use it when a department or the Mayoralty reviews the site. Nothing here moves from illustrative to verified without a named source.

Status key: **verified** has a source in `current-site.md` or `current-site-deep.md`; **illustrative** is a sample written for the prototype; **proposed** describes how the redesigned service would work and needs the service owner's agreement; **unsourced** is stated as fact on the page but has no source yet.

Last checked: 31 October 2026, against the pages and `independent-audit-2026-10-31.md`.
