# Identity and images

Research for the Amanat Baghdad case study. Gathered 30 Oct 2026 from web sources. Nothing was downloaded; this file lists URLs only.

Licenses below are copied from each source page as stated. Where a license was not visible, the entry says so.

## Summary

- **Seal.** The Amanat seal is a round badge. It shows the Round City of al-Mansur above blue Tigris waves, with "مدينة السلام" (City of Peace) inside the ring. Use it as is, small, in the header. Do not redraw it.
- **National arms.** The Eagle of Saladin is governed by Iraq's emblem law. Leave it off the Amanat site.
- **Type.** Use IBM Plex Sans Arabic for the interface and body text, with IBM Plex Sans for English. Use Noto Naskh Arabic for long reading pages. All are OFL.
- **Images.** 42 candidates are listed below. Most are Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA. The best hero options are the Tigris at dawn, the Martyrs Bridge on a Friday, and Mutanabbi Street.
- **Planning legacy.** Few open images exist. Wright and Doxiadis drawings are under archive copyright. The Commons images of the gymnasium and the university are usable with care.

## 1. Identity

### 1.1 Amanat Baghdad seal

**What it looks like** (from the Commons SVG redraw; see the sources below)

- Circular badge with a teal-blue outer ring and a white field.
- Outer ring text: "MAYORALTY OF BAGHDAD" across the top and "أمانة بغداد" across the bottom, in dark green.
- Small side text: "بغداد - العراق" on the left and "BAGHDAD - IRAQ" on the right.
- Inner roundel: "مدينة السلام" (City of Peace, the historic name of al-Mansur's Round City) in green at the top.
- Center: an oval, walled, arcaded enclosure (the Round City) with green domed buildings inside, drawn in dark indigo.
- Base: blue and white wave bands, which read as the Tigris.

**Colors** (fill values in the Commons SVG; this is a community redraw, not an official specification)

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark green | `#1b6435` | Lettering, domes |

**History and variants**

- The municipality dates from the Ottoman era (Midhat Pasha). The 1931 municipal law renamed it أمانة العاصمة (Amanat al-Asima). It is now أمانة بغداد. Source: Arabic Wikipedia.
- The seal on a white field is used as the city flag. Source: FOTW.
- **Do not confuse it with:**
  - Baghdad Provincial Council logo (مجلس محافظة بغداد): a green ring, a sand-colored pointed arch and a flame in the flag colors. This is a separate governorate body. Image cited by FOTW: <https://www.alsumaria.tv/newsimages/NB-193790-636214743436437884.jpg>
  - Amanat Baghdad SC, the football club. Its crest is separate. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanat_Baghdad_SC>
- The current site sets headings in Cairo with tatweel (أمـانة بغـداد). See `current-site.md`.

### 1.2 National coat of arms (Eagle of Saladin)

**Design (2008 to present)**

- A gold Eagle of Saladin facing the viewer's left.
- On its chest, a shield in red, white and black horizontal bands. The white band carries the Takbir (الله أكبر) in green Kufic.
- The eagle holds a scroll reading جمهورية العراق.
- Changes: 1991 (Takbir added), 2004 (Takbir set in Kufic), 2008 (stars removed, matching the flag).
- Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Iraq>
- File: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coat_of_arms_of_Iraq.svg>. Direct file: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Coat_of_arms_of_Iraq.svg>. Released to the public domain by its Commons authors. The page carries the insignia-restriction note.

**Usage rules**

- The base law is the Law on the Emblem and Seal of the Republic of Iraq, No. 85 of 1965.
- A 2017 amendment text (law number left blank on the page) does the following:
  - limits where the emblem may appear;
  - lets ministries and bodies not tied to a ministry use seals bearing the state seal, with the body's name written inside the circular frame;
  - requires a presidential permit for commercial, industrial or advertising use;
  - raises the penalties.
  Source: <https://www.mohamah.net/law/?p=176963>. The enacted text should be checked in al-Waqa'i al-Iraqiya.
- A 2010 cabinet amendment set required display locations (presidency, parliament, ministries, missions abroad): <https://www.ajnet.me/news/2010/8/3/هل-يتغير-شعار-جمهورية-العراق>
- Not verified: an academic paper mentions an "Emblem of the Republic of Iraq Law No. 2 of 2023". No primary text was found.

### 1.3 How Iraqi official bodies present themselves

- Federal ministries put the eagle emblem on their seals, with the body's name in the circular frame, as the emblem law describes.
- Local bodies use their own seals. Baghdad Provincial Council uses an arch and flag-color flame in a green ring. The Amanat uses the Round City roundel.
- The flag colors red, white, black and green are the common shared vocabulary.
- The federal e-services portal is Ur (`ur.gov.iq`), launched in September 2021: <https://www.iraq-businessnews.com/2022/09/06/baghdad-launches-ur-e-govt-portal/>
- No federal visual identity standard or web design system was found for `gov.iq` sites. This is a gap, and it is also an opening for the case study.

### 1.4 Recommendation: using the seal

1. **Header.** Put the seal at 40–48 px beside a typeset wordmark "أمانة بغداد", with "Mayoralty of Baghdad" on the English mirror. Do not use tatweel. Do not put the eagle in the header.
2. **Do not redraw or recolor the seal.** Use a clean vector traced from an official source. The Commons SVG is a community redraw and is good enough for the prototype only.
3. **One official color.** Take the seal's dark green (about `#1b6435`) as the single brand color. Use teal blue only as a secondary color for river and water content. Keep red and black for alerts, not for branding.
4. **Monochrome version.** A one-color seal (green, or white on green) for the favicon, footer and print. Check that it stays legible at 32 px; below that, use a simplified mark with only the Round City and the waves.
5. **Place the national arms only where the law requires it.** If at all, show it in the footer next to "جمهورية العراق". This prototype is private and local. Any public showing would need permission from the Amanat.

## 2. Arabic typography

### 2.1 Shortlist

Weights and licenses come from each family's `METADATA.pb` in `github.com/google/fonts`. Pairing and fit are recommendations.

| Typeface | License | Weights | Latin pairing | Fit for authority and reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (Abbink, Bold Monday, Apelian, Morcos) | OFL | 100–700, 7 static | IBM Plex Sans (same family) | Strong. Calm and institutional, with clear counters. Saudi Platforms Code lists it. **Pick for UI and body.** |
| **Noto Naskh Arabic** (Google) | OFL | Variable 400–700 | Noto Serif or IBM Plex Serif | Strongest for long reading. Traditional naskh texture with a scholarly tone. **Pick for long-form pages and "The City".** |
| **Noto Kufi Arabic** (Google) | OFL | Variable 100–900 | Noto Sans, Inter or Roboto | Geometric and formal. The UAE federal system uses it for body text. Long texts read less easily than in naskh. |
| **Alexandria** (Mohamed Gaber, Julieta Ulanovsky) | OFL | Variable 100–900 | Its own Latin, or Montserrat | Display only. The UAE uses it for headings. Strong but somewhat promotional. |
| **Readex Pro** (Jockin, Chahine, Shaver-Troup, Orozco, Gómez) | OFL | Variable 160–700, plus HEXP axis | Its own Latin (Lexend-based) | Built for reading ease and very legible. Looks friendly rather than official. Good for forms. |
| **Tajawal** (Boutros Fonts) | OFL | 200, 300, 400, 500, 700, 800, 900 | Its own Latin, or Inter | Clean and widely used. Feels generic and common on commercial sites. |

**Baseline.** The current site uses Cairo, also OFL. Moving to Plex plus Naskh sets the redesign apart and improves long-text reading.

**Arabic setting rules for the prototype**

- Body text at 18 px or larger, since Arabic at 16 px reads small.
- Line-height 1.6–1.8.
- Letter-spacing 0. No tatweel.
- Use logical CSS properties (`margin-inline-start`).

| Government of Abu Dhabi | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | The 2024 guidelines PDF has a "Primary Typeface Arabic" page (p. 30), but the file was too large to read here. **Takeaway.** Gulf systems pick one open Arabic family and pair it with a matching Latin. Plex follows the Saudi model, which also suits Iraq. The UAE's Kufi body text is the main alternative.
